GAME DEVELOPMENT

I started developing video games in 2019 and have released seven games on itch.io. During almost 7 years of experience with Unity, I've learned a lot about object-oriented programming with C#. I've also played around with Godot and learned to make code for games with GDScript. My personal goal is to one day create a game that has a significant impact on the player.

Game Design

Game design is an interesting and perhaps one of the most challenging part of my development process. When making games, I try to focus on creating engaging gameplay and combining it with a compelling story. Over the years, I've learned about building well-balanced levels, designing intuitive game systems, and iterating on feedback to refine the player's experience.
